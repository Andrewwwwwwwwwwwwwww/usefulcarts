## About

**UsefulCarts** adds a whole family of carts — hauled by you on foot or drawn by a horse, donkey, or
mule — for getting around, moving your goods, and working your fields.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial port.** UsefulCarts is a version port of **NiftyCarts** (by jmb05, itself
> a Fabric port of **AstikorCarts** by MennoMax) to Minecraft **26.2** and **26.1.2**. I did not create
> the mod — all of the original code and art belong to their authors; I've only updated it to run on the
> newest Minecraft. If you play on a version the original supports, please use it instead:
> **[NiftyCarts on Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/niftycarts)** · **[GitHub](https://github.com/jmb05/nifty-carts)**. Licensed **MIT**.

## The carts

- **Supply Cart** — a rolling double-chest you can pull on foot; it also seats a passenger and shows
  some of its cargo stacked on top.
- **Hand Cart** — a lighter, player-pulled cart with 27 slots of storage.
- **Wagon** — a large horse-drawn hauler with expandable storage (feed it chests), an optional
  wool-carpet roof, and banner decoration.
- **Animal Cart** — a two-seat cart for ferrying players or livestock; small animals that wander into
  it climb aboard on their own.
- **Plow** — load it with a hoe, shovel, or axe, then drive it across the ground to till farmland,
  carve dirt paths, or strip logs.
- **Seed Drill** — holds 9 stacks of seeds and plants them automatically as you pull it over farmland.
- **Reaper** — sit on it while a horse pulls it to mow a wide swath of crops and clear ground growth.

## How to use

Every cart hitches the same way: get within a couple of blocks of it and press the
**Attach/Detach Cart** key (**R** by default). On foot you pull it yourself; from the saddle of a
horse/donkey/mule your mount pulls it. Press the key again to unhitch. There's also a **Toggle Slow**
key (**Z**) for careful maneuvering. Place a cart by right-clicking the ground with the cart item, and
right-click storage carts to open them.

## Requirements

- **Fabric Loader** + **[Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)**
- **[Forge Config API Port](https://modrinth.com/mod/forge-config-api-port)** (by Fuzs)
- *Sodium is supported but optional.*
